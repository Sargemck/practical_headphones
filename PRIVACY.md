# Privacy policy

Practical Headphones, effective 2026-11-04.

## What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no internet permission and no analytics or crash-reporting code. Nothing leaves your phone.

## What the app stores on your phone

- **Your headphones' settings, as last read:** name, modes, noise control, equaliser, Self Voice and similar. They're shown while the headphones are away. The file sits in the app's private storage.
- **The Bluetooth address of your paired headphones,** so the app finds them again after a rename.
- **A few flags:** whether you've been asked for a permission, and whether the Quick Settings tile is added.

None of it is backed up or copied to a new phone. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Nearby devices (Bluetooth connect) | Talk to your headphones. |
| Notifications | The ongoing notification with battery, mode and quick actions. Optional. |
| Foreground service (connected device) | Keep the connection while you use the headphones. |
| Observe companion device presence, start foreground services from the background | Auto-connect: start when your headphones connect. Only after you allow it in Android's dialog. |
